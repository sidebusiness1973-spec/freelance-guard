Kids Travelers Privacy Policy

This policy describes the Kids Travelers iOS app published by Hiroshi Amano, formerly developed as Japan Buddy! – Kids Travel Japanese.

Information stored on your device

The app requires no account. Learning progress, favorites, learning mode, stars, badges and buddy choices are stored locally. The app does not ask for a child's name, date of birth, contact information or precise location.

Optional microphone and speech practice

A parent or guardian can enable optional practice recording. Recordings are temporary, limited to a short practice session, deleted when practice closes, and stale temporary files are removed at launch. Recordings are not uploaded to the developer.

Japanese word recognition is requested only where on-device recognition is supported. The app requires on-device recognition and does not fall back to server recognition. Unsupported or unavailable recognition uses recording playback instead. Recognized words are used for the practice session and are not a pronunciation score.

Parent Area and security

A parent PIN protects settings and purchase controls. PIN verification data is stored locally in the device Keychain. Device authentication can be used to reset the PIN; the app does not receive Face ID biometric data.

Purchases

Apple processes the optional Full Trip Pack purchase and purchase restoration through StoreKit. The app checks verified purchase entitlements to unlock content. The developer does not receive your payment card details. Apple's services are governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Advertising, analytics and tracking

The app does not include advertising, third-party analytics or cross-app tracking. The app does not transmit learning progress or practice recordings to a developer server.

Deleting data

A parent can delete learning progress from the protected Parent Area. This does not cancel purchases or erase Apple's transaction records. Uninstalling the app removes its app-container data; Keychain information and operating-system backups may persist according to Apple's system behavior. Temporary recordings are deleted as described above.

Children and support

The app is intended for children to use with a parent or guardian. The support website is for adults. If an adult contacts us through GitHub Issues, the submitted issue and account information are handled by GitHub and are public. Do not submit children's personal information or recordings. Support correspondence is separate from data handled by the app.

Updates and contact

This policy will be updated if the app's data practices change. Parents and guardians can contact Hiroshi Amano using the instructions on the Kids Travelers support page.
