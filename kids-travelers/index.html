Kids Travelers Support

Travel Japanese for children and families. This page is for parents and guardians.

Learning and travel phrases

Explore fixed Japanese phrases with English meanings, kana, optional romaji, picture cards, listening practice, games and a travel passport. No account is required.

40 phrases are free. The optional Full Trip Pack is a one-time purchase that unlocks all 100 phrases. Purchase and restore controls are inside the protected Parent Area.

Common questions

Audio: Check your volume and language voices. Japanese, slow Japanese and English playback use available system voices when a bundled recording is unavailable. Voice availability may vary by device and language settings.

Practice recordings: Microphone access is optional. On-device Japanese recognition is used only on supported devices. Otherwise, listen back to your voice. Recognition is a listening helper, not a pronunciation score.

Purchases: Open the Parent Area to check product availability or restore a purchase using the Apple Account that made it. Pending family approval does not unlock the pack until approved.

Progress and parent PIN: Progress is stored on your device. Parent PIN recovery uses device authentication. Deleting learning progress is a separate action in the Parent Area.

Contact the developer

Parents and guardians can contact Hiroshi Amano through GitHub Issues. Start the issue title with “Kids Travelers” and include the app version, device model, iOS version and a description of the problem.

Issues are public. Do not post children's names, practice recordings, parent PINs, Apple Account details, receipts or other personal information. If sharing a screenshot, remove personal information first. Children should ask a parent or guardian to contact support.

Kids Travelers provides fixed phrases rather than free-text translation or AI conversation. A travel phrase is not medical advice; seek qualified help when needed.
