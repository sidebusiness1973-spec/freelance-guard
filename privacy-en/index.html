FreelanceGuard Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

1. Introduction

FreelanceGuard helps freelancers organize contracts, work records, evidence notes, deadlines, and related information.

2. Data Collection

FreelanceGuard does not collect personal information for advertising or analytics. No account registration is required.

3. Local Data Storage

Contracts, work records, evidence notes, and other information entered into the app are stored locally on your device. The developer does not receive these records.

4. Export and Backup

Users can export information as PDF or CSV files and create local backups. Users are responsible for protecting and managing their exported files and backups.

5. Third-Party Websites

The app may provide links to external consultation services. These websites have their own privacy policies.

6. Data Deletion

Users can delete records within the app. Exported files must be deleted separately.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the app's functionality or data handling practices change.

8. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, please contact the developer through the official support website.
